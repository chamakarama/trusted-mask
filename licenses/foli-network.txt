Modifications: bus shapes restricted to the city study extent; coordinates
rounded to five decimals; duplicate edges merged; public zone centres snapped
to the network; shortest directed paths calculated. These paths model a
hypothetical assignment of separately protected synthetic demand. They are not
observed passenger journeys, line loads, or a valid scheduled itinerary.

The source archive SHA-256, feed metadata and exact public geometry-domain
hash are recorded in web/src/planner/data/foli-network.json. The derived network
is bundled separately from the immutable protected release. No raw journey
records or generator truth were used to construct it. Attribution is shown
on the map. No endorsement by Föli is implied.
