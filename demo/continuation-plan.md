# Mask continuation plan

Our next step is to turn the hackathon prototype into a useful everyday privacy tool. We will start with consumers, use their feedback to improve the product, and then explore corporate deployments. If we establish repeat usage and willingness to pay, Mask could become a startup.

## Start with consumer testing

Build a browser extension that fits into existing AI chats, then test it with a small group of users on everyday tasks such as drafting emails, summarising documents and asking questions about personal records. Learn whether people understand the filtering, trust the results and keep using it. Collect feedback without uploading their private source documents by default.

## Improve reliability and usability

Use those findings to improve context-dependent filtering, reduce unnecessary removals and make local inference faster and lighter. Evaluate on unseen documents, measuring both sensitive information missed and useful facts lost. Compare the usefulness of AI answers before and after filtering. Broaden the pilot once these checks show consistent improvement.

## Explore a consumer business

Test whether users would pay for convenient protection across the AI tools they already use. A free basic version with paid advanced features is one option to explore. Repeat usage, willingness to pay and the cost of supporting the product will guide whether we form a startup and pursue funding.

## Expand into corporate use

Once consumer testing establishes a useful, reliable core, work with a few companies on focused pilots, starting with workflows such as customer-support analysis and internal document summaries. Add organisation-specific policies, central deployment and reporting that does not expose raw private content. Keep processing on the device or within the company's own environment.

Corporate pilots should establish whether Mask reduces unnecessary data sharing while preserving employees' ability to get useful work done. Those results would guide a paid team product and broader integrations.

## Long-term direction

Make Mask a privacy layer across AI tools, helping individuals and organisations share only the information a task needs. Grow from observed user needs and measured performance, with consumer adoption informing the product and corporate pilots testing the business opportunity.
