# E1 privacy argument and review boundary

Experimental policy, pending Opus cross-review and E3 selection. Privacy unit: one card's entire 2025-10-06 through 2025-11-02 record set. Adjacency: add/remove one card. Not a person guarantee. For m cards/person, conservative composition is m times epsilon; m is not bounded by the production input.

Each input card is independently transformed into final count units. Public stops are unique-key validated; no registry, labels, truth, attacker tables, or whole-population statistics participate. Missing stop events are omitted only from OD (matching upstream), while recorded line/hour and daily definitions retain them. Unknown non-null stops fail closed. Unlisted lines use a predefined `unknown` bucket. OD uses weekdays excluding double taps, consecutive-boarding gaps >60 minutes, and last-to-first closure. Tied different events are ordered by stop/line; E2 must quantify any difference from upstream stable input order. Naive local times preserve supplied wall-clock semantics; no claim about exact DST elapsed journeys.

Hash ranking over canonical within-card final units selects at most 32 OD, 64 line/hour, and 64 daily contributions per card over the WHOLE period. Hashes include card-local fields only. Removing one card leaves all other cards' selection unchanged. Duplicate identical final units are interchangeable. This selection is not privacy amplification. The per-family histogram has L1 sensitivity at most its total contribution cap; it is not a per-cell cap. Integer vector Laplace from OpenDP 0.16.0 checks distances 32/64/64 against epsilon .6/.3/.1, respectively. Sequential composition is epsilon 1, delta 0. The OpenDP check covers its measurement, not our custom preprocessing proof.

Domains are all public areas squared times five bands (including zeros/self-pairs), public line roster plus unknown times 24 hours, and all 28 public dates. Output clamps signed integer draws to zero; CSV and JSON contain identical values. No exact totals, inferred individual chains, IDs, scanner samples, or random seeds enter the bundle. All E1 cells are explicitly uncertain: intervals are unavailable, not zero. No final ranking-confidence claim.

A single publisher's SQLite ledger serializes reservations before randomness. Reservations count against a total epsilon-1 allowance even on failure. A committed identical request reuses the checked bundle. An interrupted request refuses to redraw; conservatively charged budget remains. Renaming a release, changing code, or restarting cannot bypass the SAME ledger's budget. Different ledger files/machines are not coordinated: operators must retain the publisher-of-record ledger. No production enforcement against a malicious operator is claimed. Release directories and parent directories must be operator-owned; this is not a hostile shared-filesystem service.

E1 requires review before integration or formal claims. Final policy selection, 100-draw evaluation, uncertainty, benchmark adapters, inference robustness, attack demonstration, public explorer and cross-machine validation remain separate gates. Public synthetic utility evidence is not automatically safe to publish for real data.
